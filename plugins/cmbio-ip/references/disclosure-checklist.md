# Disclosure Clearance Checklist

Run this before anything technical goes outside Cmbio without an NDA.

## When to run it

Conference abstracts and posters. Talks and webinars. Preprints and papers. Grant applications. Public repository pushes and releases. Detailed sales decks and technical proposals. Website and marketing copy describing how something works. Client deliverables where the governing agreement does not impose confidentiality on Cmbio's methods. Investor materials shared without an NDA.

If in doubt, run it. It takes five minutes.

## The rule people forget

Europe has no grace period. A single public disclosure of the invention before the filing date destroys novelty for a European patent, including a disclosure made by the inventor. The US grace period is narrow and does not rescue the European position. Once it is out, it is out, and no amount of budget fixes it afterwards.
