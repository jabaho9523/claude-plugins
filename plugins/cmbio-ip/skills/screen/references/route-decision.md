## If the grid was overridden

State which cell the scorecard indicated, and why the panel went elsewhere. Common legitimate reasons: a filing made for valuation or investor purposes rather than exclusion, a defensive filing against a known competitor, a disclosure deadline forcing an early decision.

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|

Route-specific minimums:

**Patent**: priority filing route chosen (DK national / US provisional / direct), target filing date, 12-month decision date for PCT, budget approved by.

**Trade secret**: register entry created, access list defined, protection measures implemented, contract clauses that carry it (employee, contractor, sponsor).

**Defensive publication**: venue, date, who writes it, confirmation that nothing patent-worthy is bundled in.

**No action**: confirmation that the IDF is retained for tax purposes and that the inventor is released from the disclosure hold.
